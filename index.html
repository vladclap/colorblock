Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Chromigo is a casual puzzle game. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the game.

Chromigo is developed by Vladyslav Fisiak. For privacy questions or requests, email vldfsk13@gmail.com.

Information the game uses

Chromigo does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or precise location. Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device.

Advertising

Chromigo may show interstitial advertisements and optional rewarded advertisements when you choose to obtain an additional Undo or continue a game. Rewarded ads are optional; a reward is granted only when the advertising provider confirms that its reward requirements were met.

Advertising is provided by Google AdMob. Depending on your device, region, and consent choices, Google and its advertising partners may process device or app identifiers, IP address (which may be used to estimate approximate location), ad impressions, advertising interactions, product interactions, crash reports, and performance information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and maintain and improve advertising services. The game does not request GPS or precise-location permission.

Read Google’s Privacy Policy and information about partner apps and sites for details about its processing and privacy controls.

Consent and privacy choices

Where required, a consent message explains advertising purposes and partners before requesting your choices. You may decline consent without losing access to ordinary gameplay. Declining consent does not necessarily remove all advertising: limited ads may still be available, depending on applicable requirements and provider settings.

To review or change your choices, return to the game’s main menu, open Settings, and select Privacy choices when that option is available. You can withdraw previously granted consent through that form. Withdrawal applies to future processing based on consent and does not undo processing that already occurred. Availability of ads and rewarded features may vary with your choices and network conditions.

Children

Chromigo is not directed to children under the age at which consent is required in their country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and sharing

We do not operate servers that store player profiles. Saved progress, scores, and game settings remain in the app’s local storage until removed or replaced. Device backups may contain app data, depending on your Apple settings.

Advertising data is processed by Google and applicable advertising partners according to their policies. Retention varies by data type, purpose, and provider; we do not set or have direct access to all of those retention periods. See the linked provider policies, including Google’s retention information.

If you contact support, your email address, message, and any attachments are processed to respond to your request. Correspondence is kept for as long as reasonably needed to resolve the issue and related follow-up, or to meet applicable legal obligations. Our email service provider also processes this correspondence.

Deletion and privacy requests

There is no Chromigo account to delete. To remove locally stored game data from your iPhone, delete the app using Delete App rather than Offload App. This removes local progress and settings; data retained in backups is managed separately through your Apple backup settings. Deleting the app does not automatically delete data already processed by advertising providers.

You can email us to request access, correction, or deletion of information you have provided directly, including support correspondence. Please do not send identity documents or sensitive information unless specifically needed and requested. We will explain any information needed to handle your request and any applicable retention obligations.

For information held by Google or its advertising partners, use their privacy controls and request procedures described in their policies. You may also contact us for guidance, but we cannot promise to identify or erase data held independently by a provider. Depending on your location, you may have additional privacy rights, including the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game or its services change. The newest version will always be available on this page.
