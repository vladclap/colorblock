Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Chromigo is a casual puzzle game. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the game.

Information the game uses

Chromigo does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or precise location. Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device.

Advertising

Chromigo may show optional rewarded advertisements, for example when you choose to continue a game. Advertising is provided by Google AdMob. Depending on your device, region, and consent choices, Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, IP address, advertising and interaction data, and diagnostic information to deliver, measure, prevent fraud in, and improve ads.

Where required, we ask for your consent before using advertising data for personalized ads. You can manage your privacy choices through the options shown in the game. You can also review Google’s Privacy Policy.

Children

Chromigo is not directed to children under the age at which consent is required in their country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and sharing

We do not operate servers that store player profiles. Data processed by advertising providers is handled according to their own policies and retention practices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game or its services change. The newest version will always be available on this page.
