Reporting a problem

To help us understand what happened, please include:

  • Your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version.
  • The game version, if you know it.
  • What you were doing and the steps that reproduce the issue.
  • A screenshot, if it helps explain the problem.

Please do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

Ads and rewards

Ads may be temporarily unavailable. If you watched a rewarded ad but did not receive the expected Undo or Continue, tell us which action you selected and what happened after the ad closed.

Your game progress

Progress and settings are stored locally on your device. Avoid deleting the app while troubleshooting if you want to keep your saved game.
